Biography

I was born in Vinh City, a small and beautiful city in Vietnam. I cultivated my love for mathematics since my junior high school years. During that time, I won several awards in national math competitions for high school students. Later on, I pursued my Bachelor's degree in Mathematics under the talented program at the VNU University of Sciences in Hanoi. There, I completed the Bachelor's program with high distinction. Subsequently, I stayed on as a lecturer at the university for three years (2021–2023) before receiving an offer to pursue a doctoral program at UIUC.
